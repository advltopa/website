Research and Investigation

Research

Steam was launched in 2003 and was bundled with Half-life 2 by Valve and revolutionized PC gaming by bringing digital distribution, automatic updates, and community features like chat and online matchmaking to the table, becoming the biggest platform for PC games. It now hosts over 50000 games and is used by millions of players worldwide every day. One of its most useful features is its search tool, which lets the users find games by name, genre, prices and sales and ratings. Users can also browse by categories such as top sellers, new releases, and special offers, making it easy to discover new games. Other similar platforms like Epic Games and GOG also let you browse and buy games, but are less popular with Steam remaining the dominant and leaves steam widely considered as the best platform

The project

Our project is website inspired by the Steam platform. It will look and feel like Steam but won't be a real store, borrowing its clean, dark aesthetic and familiar layout to create an experience that feels intuitive and recognisable to anyone who has used the original platform before. Rather than functioning as a real store, the app will instead serve as a game library simulator, with a sign in and registration feature with a username and password stored in an external CSV file It will show a collection of games with their names and genres in a clean and organized layout. To further improve usability and make the experience more interactive, users will also be able to search through their game library using a dedicated search bar. This will allow users to quickly and efficiently locate a specific title without needing to manually scroll through the entire collection, which becomes increasingly important as the size of the library grows.
