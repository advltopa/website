Implementation and Testing

Research

During our project, we first tried using Pygubu, a WYSIWIG editor for TKinter that helps build interfaces. However, we found it too confusing and difficult to work with, so we decided to drop it and just use TKinter by itself. To help with ideas, we designed a basic GUI in paint. For our design style, we took inspiration from Steam. We liked its dark theme and clean, structured layout, so we based our interface on that look and called our version Vapour essentially a knockoff version of Steam. Overall, our design process went from struggling with a tool that wasn't right for us, to learning TKINTER at a basic level. Vapour ended up looking close to steam but with our simple touch.

Testing

Testing was carried out manually us. We focused on the login/registration screen as that was the part of the app we were most worried about for bugs. For the login and registration screen, we tested a range of inputs including valid credentials, incorrect usernames, incorrect passwords and invalid characters like comma, which would create columns in our credentials csv file. When incorrect login details were entered, the site correctly displayed an appropriate error message like "Wrong username or password" . We also verified that new users could successfully create an account via the sign up page and that existing users could sign in without issue. We were generally very pleased with the way the login screen worked well. There was one bug that was left in, which was a bug in the library search feature, where abbreviations for games don't work when you search for them in the search bar.
